How I Started

I don't wanna make this something like those movies where the main character says, “Ever since I was a kid, I was always curious about technology...” and then proceeds to build a fucking rocket at the age of 8.

Nah, that's not my story.

When I was a kid, I just had an interest in building things, like any regular kid. Opening electrical stuff, motors, wires, lights, and trying to understand how they worked. I had a little knowledge about these things too.

Now, how did I get that knowledge?

My father is an all-rounder. He's a civil worker, but he can do pretty much everything. Plumbing, carpentry, electrical work, you name it. Whenever he was working, I'd be standing nearby, helping him with whatever I could. That's basically how I learned a few things.

Then, when I was around 11, I got my first laptop.

And honestly, I thought I'd never really use it that much. But as time passed, I started getting interested in it.

I used to play games like Candy Crush, Asphalt 8, and Hill Climb Racing. And I still remember the first software I downloaded was VLC Media Player. Truly goated.

Then the interest started building up, and I discovered coding. Not because I was thinking about how apps were built or anything like that. Nah, I just wanted to look cool in my class.

Back then, there was this app called WhiteHat Jr, and their ads kept showing up on my laptop. So I got curious and decided to find out what coding actually was. This was when I was in 9th grade, around 12 years old.

The first coding lecture I watched was by Shradha Khapra. And yeah, I had a crush on her back then. Don't judge me. I watched her web development video and learned HTML and CSS. I built a few websites and even a Tic Tac Toe game.

Check out my Tic Tac Toe game

Well, I basically copied the whole code from the lecture. But hey, everyone starts somewhere. I just kept exploring after that. Later, I learned JavaScript too.

The first proper website I made was: https://gixelmc.netlify.app/

This was around the time I started playing Minecraft on my laptop. I mean, I had played Minecraft before, but then I switched to playing it on my own laptop. Btw, that laptop had some Intel Pentium processor, 4GB of RAM, and 128MB of shared graphics memory. Not exactly a beast, but it did the job.

I used to play A LOT of Minecraft back then. And man, I had so many friends on Discord. Playing all day after school, joining SMPs, hanging out with people online. Oh my lord, I still remember those days. Those were the days.

And somewhere along the way, my interest in coding kept growing. I started exploring more things, learning new stuff, and building whatever I could. I still play Minecraft, btw.

So yeah, that's pretty much how I started. I really like my journey so far. I'm still learning, still improving, and still trying to figure things out. Although, I'm kinda stuck these days. But I'll find a way out. That's just how it goes, I guess.
